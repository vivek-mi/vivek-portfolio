Jira-Style Issue Tracker

The team's own work tracker for planning and shipping client projects. Work is organised as Projects → Iterations → Functionalities → Issues, and every issue carries a developer, a tester and a full assignment trail.

  • Four-level work hierarchy with project keys, project leads and linked clients
  • Story and Bug issues with priority, due date and an In Progress → In Review → Done flow
  • Separate developer and tester assignment, reassignment with a required reason, and full assignment history
  • Dashboard charts, Excel and PDF export, and a secure store for project credentials and documents

Lead & Client Management Portal

The CRM behind the StartWorks business centre. It captures enquiries from the website and from conference bookings, moves each lead through a defined workflow, and tracks the clients it converts.

  • Lead workflow from Not Contacted to Contacted or Lost, with source and status tracking
  • Leads captured from the website contact form and a public conference-booking form
  • Filters by source and status with live totals, plus PDF export
  • Client tracking for conference and virtual clients, including board-room and conference-room bookings
